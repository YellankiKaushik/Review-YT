Remotion 4.0.539 is source-available under the upstream Remotion license, not an unconditional MIT tool. The current official terms offer a free license to eligible individuals/small companies up to three people; larger organizations need to review the company-license requirements before reuse. No paid license was purchased or represented as authorized. See https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing and the installed package LICENSE. This personal local production assumes the individual-user context supplied in the brief. React, TypeScript, Playwright, Sharp, FFmpeg and FFprobe retain their respective licenses and bundled notices in node_modules; copying tool binaries has separate obligations. The deliverables are rendered media, not a redistribution of the toolchain or model weights. A future software bundle must preserve appropriate notices and comply with each distribution's terms.

No stock footage, third-party music, customer images, raw public comments, cloud-generated notes, generated voice or live YouTube thumbnails were sourced. Original V1 media and sources remain preserved locally, outside this V2 publication. Official Remotion guidance was consulted; downloaded reference copies remain local and are not redistributed in this campaign.

All assets used in the final compositions are enumerated and hashed in asset-manifest.json. No API keys or private configuration are required for rendering.
